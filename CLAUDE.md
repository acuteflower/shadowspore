# Shadowspore

The game's name is **Shadowspore** (formerly "Voidbloom"). The plant kind `voidbloom` ("Leerenblüte", assets/props/voidbloom*.png) is an unrelated in-game flower and keeps its id.

2D pixel-art top-down (3/4) action-RPG: farming, base-building, night-survival. Godot 4.7, GDScript. 1-4 players. PC first (KB/M + gamepad), iOS later. Design is still being worked out step by step with the user; don't build systems that haven't been discussed.

**Design:** how characters, items and map objects should look, work and be collected is in `docs/design_guide.md` (palettes, scale, pipelines, checklists). Read it before adding any.

## Multithreading (`Jobs`, src/autoload/jobs.gd)
- **One central job system** (autoload `Jobs`, wraps `WorkerThreadPool`): `Jobs.run(work, on_done, prio, on_discard, tag)`; `work` runs on a worker, `on_done(result)` always on the main thread (delivered in `_process`, before everything else). Priorities `URGENT/HIGH/NORMAL/LOW`, FIFO inside one; at most `max_running` (cores / 3, 1..6) jobs run at once. `Jobs.wait(job)` gives the result now (a job that has not started is computed by the main thread itself, so waiting never deadlocks), `Jobs.cancel(job)` (true = a worker had started: `on_discard(result)` cleans up later), `Jobs.drain()`, `Jobs.parallel_for(count, body)` (blocking, each call writes only its own slot), `Jobs.warm(paths)`. `--no-threads` (or `Jobs.threaded = false`) runs one job per frame on the main thread with identical results.
- **Rules for worker code:** read only what is immutable after start (DataRegistry, `Relief`, maps, SDFs, noise) or data the job owns; nodes may be created and built but **never added to the scene tree**; no shared dictionaries/arrays written, no signals of tree nodes, no `get_tree()`. Scenes/scripts a job instantiates must be loaded once on the main thread first (`Jobs.warm`): the first load inside a worker can deadlock against the main thread. Shared caches that workers touch need a mutex (`WorldRivers._query_lock`).
- **Chunk streaming** (`WorldLevel`): phases 0-4 of a `ChunkBuild` (prop query, ground rows, cliff colliders, stone beds / forest-bed candidates / prop nodes, edited props) run as ONE worker job per chunk (`GEN_PHASES`, `_gen_work`) with a forked `PropGenerator` (`fork()` / `sync_inputs()`, pool guarded by `_gen_lock`; each has its own cache and RNG). `_stream` prefetches the nearest `PREFETCH` missing chunks (all of them in `all` mode). Back on the main thread `_filter_prepared` drops props that clash with the live world (`_things`, town `cleared`), records saplings in `_young` and places forest beds after the `Terrain.patches` check; phases 5-9 (detail candidates, logs, clover, meadow details) and the atomic commit stay on the main thread under the frame budget. A chunk dropped mid-job is freed by `_gen_discard`. New heavy generation: add a pure function, run it through `Jobs`, apply the result on the main thread. Test `tools/test_jobs.tscn` (23 checks).

## Terrain
Tiles carry a `surface` custom-data string (TileSet layer "surface"); speed multipliers per surface live in `data/terrain.json`. Ground `TileMapLayer`s call `Terrain.register_layer()`; later layers override earlier ones. To add a surface: add it to the JSON, set the tile's custom data in the TileSet. Fields per surface: `speed`, `step` {kind: puff|splash, color, amount}, `footprint` {color, seconds}, `submerge` (px of water over the legs), `ripples`, `allow_sprint`, `allow_blink` (all optional).

## Day and night
- `TimeManager` (autoload): a 12-minute day (8 day incl. one minute of dawn and dusk, 4 night; 0.5 = noon; `NIGHT_START` 5/6, `NIGHT_END` 1/6). The host advances it and sends it every 2 s (`_sync_time`), clients count on in between. `ambient_color()` (tint keys in `KEY_COLORS`), `night_amount()` (0 day .. 1 night), `is_night()`. F4 (host) jumps an hour (`debug_time` action).
- `DayNight` (a `CanvasModulate` in the level) tints the world and calls `set_night` on the group `night_lights`; it also feeds `ambient` and `night` to the ground shader. HUD layers are not tinted. Anything that must stay bright (texts, bars, warning zones, loot, coins, glowing parts) uses `Unlit.material()` or `render_mode unshaded`; unshaded items ignore the tint (measured).
- Light sources are `NightLight` nodes (PointLight2D; a flat, irregular, smooth ellipse cookie from `LightTexture.apply(light, width, squash, variant)` (continuous fall-off, `SUPERSAMPLE` 2 texels per art pixel with `texture_scale` 0.5: the old six-step Bayer cookies at one texel per art pixel showed as blocks at the 2x window), drawn into a square texture because non-square cookies got clipped; every PointLight2D uses `apply`, not `get_texture`; the screen-wide light shaders (`night_moon`, `night_fog`, `sun_glow`) are continuous at screen resolution with a hair of per-pixel noise against banding, and `ground_tone` blends fractionally between the ramp greens (`SOFT` = softness of the patch edges) with only the grass blades staying crisp whole-step pixels; `radius`, `squash`, `base_energy`, `glow_color`; they breathe slowly and only shine at night). The player carries no light. Luminous plants shine themselves: `tools/art_grass.py` writes `grass_glow.png`/`glow_tileset.tres` with only the glowing pixels of bloom/mushroom/glyph tiles, the level draws them on the unshaded `Glow` layer (`glow_layer.gdshader`: brighter and breathing at night) and puts one `NightLight` per such tile (`GLOW_KINDS` in `test_level.gd`). Spiders have a subtle `NightLight`; their additive `Glow` node and `Zone` are unshaded, `ShadowPatch` is an unshaded shader.
- `Fireflies` (level node): 56 tiny blinking unshaded lights that live only inside the camera view (+40 px), 70 % hovering around glowing plants (group `flora_glow`, filled by `_add_glow`), fading in with `night_amount()`. Cosmetic, every peer runs its own.
- Design rules for lighting new things: `docs/design_guide.md` section 6.

## World map (in progress)
- Plan: one fixed, hand-authored open world of about 1100x1100 tiles (about 2 min of sprinting from the middle to the edge), the whole story happens on it. The player spawns near the middle of the map (see the start point in the world level notes), no longer at the edge; a tutorial path is not planned for now. **Difficulty is the same everywhere** (the current world uses one meadow biome with named areas; difficulty does NOT grow with distance from the start); it rises with the night instead (details to be designed). Some regions may still be locked until story progress (barriers on their borders, host-owned story flags; not built yet).
- `RegionMap` (src/world/region_map.gd, `RefCounted`, built from a seed, deterministic on every peer): the land is a noise blob (no fixed outline: big lobes pulled down at the square's edges, only the biggest connected piece kept; small inland water bodies are possible). A jittered hex grid of seeds (150 tiles apart, uneven sizes) is cut into regions by noise-warped Voronoi borders; regions below `MIN_AREA` are dropped. `region_at(tile)` / `super_at(tile)` look up a 4-tile cell grid; `regions[i]`: `center`, `neighbors`, `depth` (steps from `start_region`, a base for unlocking, not for difficulty), `area`, `super`.

The game plays on the file: `RegionMap.new(0, DataRegistry.table("world_map"))` (4 ms; generating from a seed takes about 1 s). No relief/coast/water regeneration is needed for this change. Details and previews: `docs/map_and_lakes.md`; regression `tools/test_shared_biome.tscn`.

Each chunk is painted from a seed of its own (chunk coords + map seed), so load order never matters and every peer paints the same ground itself. `meadow` gets the full meadow (same zones/glow as the arena); the other biomes are placeholders: plain grass on their own `TileMapLayer` tinted with the biome's `color`, with a few tufts. Proper shore tiles and biome-transition tiles are not built yet. **The start point** is the tile `"start"` in `data/world_map.json` (`RegionMap.start`, falls back to `anchor(start_region)` when absent; `tools/world_data.py` `start_tile`): (463, 616), a meadow hollow just south of the map's middle (550, 550), a small flat clearing under a crystal-veined stone wall (north) with a ridge to the east and firs to the west, the mist forest a short walk north. `start_region` (30) stays what depth and the biome tiers were derived from; the spawn lies in region 21 (depth 2). ## Height levels (plateaus)
- Fixed levels (0..3) above the ground, baked by `tools/bake_relief.py` (Python + numpy, needs `assets/world/land_sdf.png` and `data/world_map.json`; takes seconds, rerun after changing the map, biome `relief` blocks or the script; then `Godot --headless --import`). Per biome `data/biomes.json` has `relief` {`gain`, `bias`, `styles` weights [grass, rock, masonry, crystal, void]}; the relief is flat at the coast (7-15 tiles) and in a small clearing around the start.
- Outputs: `relief.png` (per tile R level, G kind | style << 4 (0 floor, 1 wall, 2 ramp, 3 stairs, 4 rock steps; 0 grass, 1 rock, 2 masonry, 3 crystal, 4 void, 5 Hexenwald slate), B connector geometry: bits 0-1 row, 2-3 depth - 1, 4-5 column, 6-7 the way down (0 south, 1 north, 2 east, 3 west), A 200 + biome), `relief_field.png` (the level masks >= 1, 2, 3 blurred, so a 0.5 threshold gives **smooth round contours**, no tile steps), `relief_block.png` (collision at 4 px cells) and `data/relief.json` (connector list `[x, y, kind, width, dir]`).
- Rule shared by the shader and the collision bake: a **wall is 32 px tall and hangs below every contour** (every pixel with a higher level within 32 px straight above it); the other contours get thin rims (3 px) and an edge-on side strip. The picture is `ReliefLayer` (a camera-following `ColorRect`, z -8, `assets/shaders/relief.gdshader`: wall styles, lips, shadows, ramps/stairs by tile). `Relief` (src/world/relief.gd) answers questions: `blockers(rect)` (chunk collision, merged rectangles, added to the chunk's StaticBody), `level_at(pos)` (smooth level, fraction on connectors), `flat_around(pos, r)` (props stay clear, `PROP_MARGIN` 12 px), `wall_above`, `surface_at` (`slope` / `stone` footstep surfaces via `Terrain.relief`).
- **Connectors** are the only ways up (3 tiles wide, whole tiles in relief.png): `RAMP` (3 deep, a dirt/scree path with embankments), `STAIRS` (masonry walls) and rock `STEPS` (2 deep). They lead down in all four directions: south ones cut into the 2-tile wall face, **north, east and west ones cross a plain cliff edge** (`side_tiles`, `carve_side`; the shader draws every connector in a canonical south-facing frame, `connector_frame`, and fades side ramps into the high ground instead of a patch). The baker carves a straight 7-wide stretch into a contour where it places one, links all walkable pieces with a union-find (`settle`: connect, merge cut-off pieces, repeat) so everything is reachable from the start, then `extras()` adds loops and more ways onto every plateau (also from other sides), settles again, and flattens what cannot be reached. Connectors in the wall are never blocked (the bake leaves them open). The report counts them per direction.
- Nothing on the map is hand-placed any more (the old height showcase north of the start is gone): the whole map comes from the noise. The relief per biome is tuned wild (`data/biomes.json` `relief` gain 1.15-1.35, bias -0.04..0, noise scale 50 tiles in the baker: about 30-40 % of the land lies above level 0, with plateaus up to level 3, 462 connectors: 166 ramps, 73 stairs, 223 rock steps; 147 lead down south, 110 north, 107 east, 98 west), flat only at the coast and in the 12 x 12 tiles around the start (`START_RECT`, fading out over `START_FADE` 8 tiles; props stay away from 17 x 17 tiles, `cleared` in `WorldLevel`). Props avoid walls and edges (`PropGenerator` asks `Relief.flat_around`), rubble gathers at cliff feet.
- **Glowing wall veins**: the violet veins and crystal shards of crystal walls and the magenta cracks of void walls glow and pulse at night (a little by day). `ReliefLayer` has a child `ColorRect` that runs a copy of `relief.gdshader` (the source with `render_mode unshaded, blend_add;` put in at run time, uniform `glow_pass` true): `wall_glow_pixel` / `vein_glow` mirror the vein pattern of `wall_color` (change both together); the light runs along the veins and the wall breathes slowly, each vein pixel gets a one pixel halo, strength `0.14 + 0.86 * night`.
- Not built yet: walking down by hopping off ledges, blink up onto ledges, enemy pathing around cliffs, locked regions as cliff barriers, town layouts with stairs.

## Wildlife (wild rabbits)
- Peaceful ambient wildlife is in `src/entities/wildlife/`; definitions in `data/wildlife.json`. `WildRabbit` has no hurtbox, loot or combat. The first habitat is green `meadow` (JSON `biomes`), safe flat land, away from the void, water and walls.
- **Host-authoritative:** the host alone senses threats and chooses hops, moods and landings. `WildlifePopulation` owns a `MultiplayerSpawner`; spawn properties (coat, home, temperament and position) use a `MultiplayerSynchronizer`. ## Plants and interaction
- `Interactable` (src/entities/props): base of everything E can use. It is in group `interactables`; the local player (`Player._update_interact`) picks the nearest one within `INTERACT_RANGE` (16 px beyond its radius) whose `prompt()` is not empty, it shows an `InteractPrompt` ("E VERB", unlit, above it), and E (`InputManager.interact_pressed()`) calls `interact(player)`; what has consequences is asked of the host.
- `Bush` (plain `bush` and `berry_bush`, data in `data/plants.json`: textures, per-variant radius/height, placement density, slow factor, berries, harvest). Art: `tools/art_bush.py` (sheets `assets/props/bush*.png`, plant item icons) and `tools/art_stone.py` (tools, stone, pebbles, rocks). Walk-through: a `Foliage` Area2D (mask = player) tells the player's own machine to slow down (`Player.set_slow/clear_slow`, the slowest wins) and rustles the bush everywhere. The bush's `HarvestHurtbox` (layer 5) lets weapon hit scans find it and hands the hit to the world.
- State lives in the world: `WorldLevel` keeps `_bush_states` (cell -> `{"p": day berries were picked + 1, "h": harvest slots used}`, absent = untouched; `h` >= 3 = stripped) on every peer; the host changes it (`_host_pick`, `harvest_hit`), tells everyone with `_rpc_bush_state(cell, state, fx)` and sends the table to a joining peer (`_rpc_bush_snapshot`). Bushes never disappear: every sunrise (`TimeManager.day_started`, host) `_grow_bushes` gives each plucked bush one harvest slot back per day (`harvest.regrow_days`) and brings picked berries back after 3 days (`berries.regrow_days`). Bushes are placed per chunk (`_maybe_bush`, deterministic) and freed with the chunk.
- Picking: E at a ripe berry bush -> `WorldLevel.request_pick` -> host validates distance and state -> `DropComponent.spawn_drops` (berries pop out, magnet picks them up). Harvesting: tools (`sickle_basic`: `tool: "sickle"`, `pickaxe_basic`: `tool: "pickaxe"`, kind `tool`, hotbar items, weapon classes `sickle_1h` / `pickaxe_1h`) hit the fan; `harvest_hit` checks the held item's `tool` (`InventoryComponent.active_stack()`) against the prop's `harvest.tool`, any other weapon only rustles it. Drops use the enemy loot format and are meant to be scarce (about 3 items per bush). Berries (`dusk_berry`, kind consumable, hotbar) are edible, see below.

## Eating (consumables)
- An item with `kind: "consumable"` and `stats` {`heal`: now, `regen`: more over `regen_seconds`} is used with the inventory op `"use" [slot, 0]` (host: `InventoryComponent._use`): `HealthComponent.heal` now, `HealthComponent.regen` (one tick a second, regens stack) and one item less. Healing items are not used up when nothing can be healed (`HealthComponent.can_heal`: full health and full overheal, or dead). The dusk berry heals 5 and regens 5 over 5 s. Everyone sees a green `+N` (white for overheal; `HealthComponent.healed`).
- **Overheal**: healing past `max_health` becomes overheal, up to `overheal_ratio` (default 10 %, `ceiling()`); it absorbs damage first, stays for `OVERHEAL_SECONDS` (60) after it was last topped up (`overheal_left`, counted on every peer) and then drains at `OVERHEAL_DECAY` (2 points a second, host). `HealthBar` (the red sap vine) grows a white branch out of its tip, longer with more overheal, with a glowing bud, flash on top-up and withering in the last 10 seconds; the label shows the real value.
- Triggers (local player): E eats from the selected hotbar slot when no interactable is in reach (an interactable's prompt wins, so E at a ripe berry bush picks); pressing the number key of the already selected slot uses it too (`Hotbar.use_requested`). Both call `Player.use_slot`, which plays the one-second eating animation (weapon put away; **she can walk while she eats**, not sprint: starting it drops a sprint into a walk, and sprint, sneak, blink and swings are blocked until it ends; the eat sheet has the 7 leg variants of the attack sheet, `EAT_VARIANTS`, so the legs keep the walk cycle while the upper body plays; picking still plants her) and sends the `"use"` op at the first bite (`EAT_USE_TIME` 0.42 s); a hit before that interrupts it and the berry is kept, at full health nothing starts.
- `TimeManager.day` / `day_started(day)`: a new day begins at sunrise (06:00, `DAY_START`) whenever the clock moves forward past it (also by cheat or F4); the host's count is synced. Use it for anything that grows back by days.

## Tools, stones and rocks
- **A tool in the selected hotbar slot replaces the weapon in her hand**: `InventoryComponent.active_stack()` (a `kind: "tool"` item in slot `selected`, else the equipped main-hand weapon). The owner picks the slot (`Hotbar._select` -> `InventoryComponent.select`, told to the host with the `"select"` op), `Player._on_inventory_changed` sets `attack.held_weapon` from it (so every peer sees the tool in her hand), `AttackComponent._server_swing` and `WorldLevel.harvest_hit` use it. Tools are normal inventory items (`hotbar: true`, no equipment slot; the loadout may fix a slot: `"slot": n`; sickle in slot 1, pickaxe in 2, slot 0 stays free). Tools swing like any weapon (their `weapon_class` combo, `stats`), so they also hit enemies a little.
- `WorldProp` (base of `Bush`, `Pebble`, `Rock`; src/entities/props) holds what they share: level, cell, kind, variant, the synced state ({"h": slots used, "p": day picked + 1, "k": kind}), spec from `data/plants.json`, `slots()`, `is_depleted()`, `harvest_hit`, rustle and burst effects. `WorldLevel` keeps `_things` (loaded props), `_thing_states` (synced), places them per chunk from `PropGenerator` (`_place_props`) and regrows them at sunrise (`_grow_things`: one harvest slot a day per `harvest.regrow_days`, picked things after `pickup.regrow_days`).
- **`PropGenerator`** (src/world/prop_generator.gd) decides what stands where, as a pure function of the world seed, the ground noise (`_zone`: wild = shaded ground) and the baked coast. A **background** keeps the whole meadow populated and even (one candidate per cell of a jittered grid: `lone_tree`, `lone_bush`, `lone_stone` = single pebbles and now and then a single small rock), and **clusters** sit on top: groves of firs (giants in the middle, smaller toward the edge, bushes between), thickets, and **rock formations, the only place stones lie in groups**: each has its own bed of gravel (a `Sprite2D` decal from `stonebed.png`, 6 sizes, `z_index -9`, spawned with the chunk by `WorldLevel._add_stone_bed`, kind `stonebed` in the generator output) and a big rock, 2-5 smaller ones and pebbles only on that bed (`_on_bed` flattens the bed for the 3/4 view). Cluster cells (`LAYERS`) hold a deterministic cluster each (cached). **Nothing overlaps**: every candidate has a footprint (`_foot`: trunk/rock/bush radius; trees also a crown radius, crowns may only touch, `CROWN_OVERLAP` 0.8) and a priority (tree, rock, berry bush, bush, pebble, plus a seeded tie-break); a candidate is dropped when a higher-priority candidate overlaps it, which only looks at nearby candidates (a bucket grid, `BUCKET` 72 px), so any chunk gets the same answer. `edge_margin` and the cleared start area (37 x 37 tiles around the start) are applied when candidates are made. `position_of(cell)` gives a prop's feet position. Rocks are 8 variants built from the same faceted blocks (lit cap, mid left face, dark right face, chipped corners) so they belong together: boulders, a big mossy one, a rune stele, a slab, a group of stone pillars, a crystal rock; `rock.png` 64x56 frames, feet (32, 52).
- **Pebbles** (little stones): E picks one up (`pickup`: item `stone`, the pick animation, back after 3 days), more of them along shores. **Rocks**: solid (`StaticBody2D` footprint), 4 variants with 3-5 slots, mined with the pickaxe (each hit drops stone with a chance, the last gives 1-2 more), whole -> chipped -> small -> rubble (walkable), one slot back per 2 days.
- **Trees** (`FirTree`, `tree.tscn`, kind `tree` in plants.json, art `tools/art_tree.py` + `tools/art_spruce.py`): 4 firs (84, 124, 164, 192 px; frames 112x200, feet (56, 196)); crooked, tiered boughs with ragged drooping fingers, hanging luminous moss, lanterns, shelf fungi, violet-veined bark, violet void-touched needle tips. **Sheet rows 4:** 0 trunk, 1 crown, 2 crown glow, 3 trunk glow; the glow rows hold only the emissive pixels (plus soft halos) and are drawn in child sprites (`Sprite/Glow`, `Crown/Glow`) with the `Unlit` material: alpha 0.22 by day, 1.0 at night (`FirTree.set_night`, called by `DayNight` through the group `night_lights`), and they fall with the tree; variants 1-3 also carry a teal `NightLight` pool; the crown sways in the wind at all times (`IDLE_SWAY`). To change the look: edit the `VARIANTS` / drawing functions in art_spruce.py, run `python3 tools/art_tree.py`, then `Godot --headless --import`. the big ones have a bare trunk under a high crown (clear heights 38/62/72 px, a person is 52 px) so you can walk under them. The trunk is solid (small `StaticBody2D`), the crown is a second sprite (sheet row 1) on top of the trunk sprite (row 0) that, **together with the trunk, the glow and the ground shadow (the whole tree)**, fades to 28 % **only while it really hides a player**: `FirTree._covers` tests, for every player within 260 px who stands behind the tree (his feet above the tree's base on the screen), nine points of his body (3 heights x 3 widths) against the trunk (`trunk_half_width`, up to the crown's start `clear`) and the crown shape (`plants.json` `crown_shape`: `cone` = firs, widest at the bottom; `dome` = an ellipse, blossom trees; `curtain` = willows, strands hanging straight down from a dome of `dome_height`); standing under it, beside the trunk or in front never fades it (every player counts, so you see friends too; all variants have `crown_fade` true). Chopped with the axe (`axe_basic`, `tool: "axe"`, class `axe_1h`, grip (4, 9), hotbar slot 3): 4/6/8/8 hits, each drops wood with a chance (the last: 2-3 wood and a rare resin), the last topples the tree (it tilts and fades) and leaves a stump; state `{"h": slots, "f": 1}`; `f` blocks chopping until it is whole again, one slot back every `regrow_days` (2): stump -> sapling -> young tree -> fir (`tree_growth.png`, chosen by progress). **Chop animation** (`FirTree`): every hit shakes the tree (`_shake` / `_sway`: a damped sway, the crown far (rotation 0.05 rad plus skew), the trunk hardly, first away from the nearest player), shakes needles loose (`_needle_rain`: a CPUParticles2D over the crown that drifts down) and chips bark at the trunk; the last hit starts `_fell` (the state is already felled, `_falling` blocks other state changes until the end): the trunk cracks and shudders (0.32 s, bark and 28 needles fly), then `_cut_through` swaps the trunk for the **stump, which stays with its shadow**, and only the part above the cut (the trunk above the stump height plus the crown, two `AtlasTexture` sprites in a node hinged at the cut, `_fall`) topples away from the hitter with an ease-in (so it speeds up, 0.9 s + height/230) to 1.42 rad, impacts (`_impact`: dust, needles, `Player.add_shake` for players within 360 px), bounces once, lies 0.7 s, fades 0.5 s and is removed. Collision and hurtbox are off from the start of the fall. Trees stand in groves (see `PropGenerator`).

## Story plan of the opening (told by the user, built step by step; nothing of it beyond the intro and the wounded state exists yet)
1. Intro: she crawls out of the sewer wounded (built). While wounded (<= 10 % health) she is slow, limps, cannot sprint (built).
2. A **quest system** leads her along the footpath to find help (the quest system does not exist yet).
3. Early on, beside the path, **shadow spiders** stand close enough to attack anyone who simply walks past and **kill her at once**: this is where sneaking is taught, she has to sneak by.
4. Further on: a **dead knight (?)**, on him she finds a very simple **sword** (first weapon).
5. A **crossroads**: the quest makes her read the **signpost**. Reading it starts a half **cutscene**: **3 shadow spiders attack**; she can dodge and fight, but the fight must be **100 % unwinnable** (guaranteed).
6. If she dies there she does **not respawn**. Instead an **adventurer/knight** arrives, kills the spiders and saves her; shortly after she **passes out**. (Last thing to build: much animation.)
7. She wakes up in **his hut**: that is where the game really starts, from there she builds her **town** etc.
