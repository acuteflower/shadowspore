# Boss arena (stage for the first boss fight)

Stage only: the boss design comes from another agent. Plateau at tile (499, 258) (relief level 3, ramp on the south side).

- **Data:** `data/ground.json` `arenas[]` {id, tile, level, clear_margin, poly (px relative to the tile's centre, traced from the user's drawing), pillars [{pos, variant}]}.
- **Floor:** `tools/bake_ground.py --yards` (seconds) bakes `assets/world/ground/boss_arena_arena.png` + mask (surface `stone`) with the same flagstone look as the start field, grass/moss breaking through. It is cut to the plateau top (relief field channel `level - 1`, 4 px away from the lip). Then `Godot --headless --path . --import`.
- **Empty stage:** `WorldLevel._add_arenas` keeps every tile inside the polygon (+ `clear_margin` px) free of generated trees, bushes and plants.
- **Pillars:** `ArenaPillar` (`src/entities/props/arena_pillar.gd`), art `py -3 tools/art_arena_pillars.py` -> `assets/props/arena_pillars{,_glow}.png` (3 variants, frames 64x128, feet (32, 118), contact sheet `docs/img/arena_pillars.png`). Solid 22x8 footprint, glowing vein (unlit, brighter at night), violet `NightLight`, fades to 38 % while it hides someone behind it. Two pillars were moved inward from the drawn spots so their feet stand fully on the plateau top.
- Preview: `Godot --rendering-driver opengl3 --path . tools/preview_daylight.tscn -- --dir=/abs --times=0.5 --at=499,258`.

## Overhaul: floor design and decoration
- **Floor design** (`tools/arena_design.py`, called by `bake_arena`; `python3 tools/bake_ground.py --yards` then `Godot --headless --import`): a consecrated sun sigil of the sun eagle's order (hub with a lit core, grooved rings, sixteen rays, a rune band, fine outer rays, a dashed thread of light), a round slab with a rune ring under every pillar, a ley line from each pillar to the sigil, violet cracks of the void running out of its middle, a chequered frieze a hand's width inside the lip; worn in patches, moss over it near the lip, no grass hole in the sigil or under a pillar. Circles are squashed 0.7 (3/4 view). The inlaid light is also baked as `boss_arena_arena_glow.png`, drawn unlit by `GroundDecal` (dim by day, alive at night). Light rubble (chips, grit) lies at the pillars' feet, along the lip and in drifts.
- **Decoration, kept small on purpose:** only light rubble and the remains of plants. Chips and grit are baked into the floor (at the pillars' feet, along the lip, in drifts), dry tufts, fallen leaves and moss are baked in too (`arena_design.plant_remains`), and six small rubble heaps stand on it (`data/ground.json` `arenas[].decor`, one `HutYard` each, art `py tools/art_arena_decor.py` -> `assets/props/arena/`). The script still makes banners, fire bowls, stumps, plinths, drums, feathers and crystals, but none of them is placed (the user found that too much): add entries to `decor` only if asked.

## Fight zone and pillar slabs (fix)
- **Fight zone:** the warnings and the damage used the flagstone mask, so every grass hole between the stones and the green rim of the plateau were safe. `bake_ground.py` now also bakes `boss_arena_arena_zone.png` (`bake_arena_zone`: the whole plateau top, 2 px inside the lip, the piece connected to the drawn polygon; `ground_baked.json` `zone`, `zone_x`, `zone_y`) and `FeatherField.setup` reads that (the flagstone mask still drives footsteps). The rift also runs to the plateau's edge now.
- **Pillar slabs:** each slab is centred on the pillar's footprint (5 px behind its feet row), is raised (a dark front edge), and a contact shadow lies round the plinth, so the pillar stands on it instead of looking pasted on a flat circle. The frieze and the lip-side rubble used the wrong sign of the distance field before (`sd` is negative inside the lip) and were drawn outside the floor.
