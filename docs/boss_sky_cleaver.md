# Boss 1: Mornath, der Himmelsspalter (`boss_sky_cleaver`)

HUD name `MORNATH, DER HIMMELSSPALTER`. Once a consecrated sun eagle of the order of the High Spire (Valdric's order), eaten by the Void: marrow replaced by drawing vacuum, plumage fused into razor chitin lamellae, the old bronze harness grown into the flesh, its protective runes now cramping violet veins. Sits on the pillars of the arena (docs/boss_arena.md). Phases 1 and 2 of the fight are built (below); of phase 3 the start (push, scream, fire) is built.

## Look (shared shadow-creature DNA)
- Black flesh `07040d`-`221740`, outline `040208`, cold rim `5a36b8`; basalt beak/horn/blade lamellae like the crusher; emissive layer through `VoidGlow` (six magenta eye slits, violet wing veins pulsing along the blades, white-violet resonance core in the breastbone, harness runes). No smoke.
- Raptor skull with basalt beak, two-segment blade wings (6 primary, 5 secondary blades joined by violet membranes), 4-6 void tendrils that trail physically. Wingspan 78-88 px in flight, 46-49 px perched.
- `SkyCleaverRig` (`sky_cleaver_rig.gd`): 100 % procedural pixel geometry like `CrusherRig`. Poses `PERCHED` (gargoyle crouch, wings folded forward-down, breathing, head scanning, a void bead falls now and then), `TAKEOFF`, `FLYING`, `HOVER`, `LANDING`, `STAGGER`, `CRASHED`, `DEAD`. Preview/contact sheet: `tools/preview_sky_cleaver.tscn -- --capture=/abs.png` (`docs/img/sky_cleaver.png`). Sounds: `py -3 tools/audio_sky_cleaver.py` (`assets/audio/sky_cleaver_*.wav`, placeholders).

## Behaviour (`src/entities/enemies/sky_cleaver.gd`, data `data/enemies.json` `boss_sky_cleaver`)
- States `PERCHED` (dwell 5-8 s) -> `TAKEOFF` (0.6 s windup) -> `FLYING` (quadratic Bezier between two pillars, rises 72 px above the perches, eased) -> `HOVER` (0.9-1.6 s above the target) -> `LANDING` (0.5 s braking flare, springy touchdown, small tremor) -> `PERCHED`. `STAGGER` when a heavy hit (knockback >= `heavy_knockback`) breaks the take-off. Death: `CRASHED` (falls to the ground) then `DEAD`.
- 2.5D: the root `CharacterBody2D` always stays on the ground (no collision, y-sort, ground shadow 36x14 px, smaller/fainter with height); `Rig.position.y = -roundf(height)`, `Rig` has `z_index 5`. Perch heights per pillar variant: `ArenaPillar.PERCH_HEIGHTS`.
- **Host authority, deterministic replay:** the host decides and sends reliable RPCs (`_rpc_fly_to_pillar`, `_rpc_phase`, `_rpc_perch_land`, `_rpc_ground_crash`); every peer plays the same timeline, nothing about its position is synchronised; a joining peer asks for a snapshot (`_request_state`). Spawn properties: `arena`, `pillar_index`, `enemy_id`, scales.
- Hittable (hurtbox on the ground at the pillar's foot) only while perched, taking off, landing and staggering; not in the air.
- Boss bar: every peer tracks it for its own local players within `bar_range` (300 px) of the arena centre (`BossBar.track/release`).
- **Entrance (the fight begins):** nothing of the boss exists until the story calls him: `WorldLevel.request_arena_boss(arena_id, from)` (any peer asks, the host summons; from the quest `mornath` after the inspection or from the hologram, see docs/boss_arena.md "Quest"). Standing on the floor no longer wakes him. Then `_summon_arena_boss` spawns it **off-screen**: `arrival.distance` (460 px) past its starting pillar (`arenas[].boss.pillar`), seen from the one who stepped in, pushed farther until it is >= 430 px from every player; spawn property `arrive_height` (> 0 = arriving; data `arrival.height` 210) and `position` are all peers need. It swoops in (`_arrival`: FLYING eased out = already at speed, then it brakes; `arrival` {speed, arc, curve, hover} in data/enemies.json) -> HOVER -> LANDING -> PERCHED (`first_dwell`), not hittable in the air, a distant take-off cry plays at the start. **The boss bar appears at the touchdown** (`_update_bar` waits for `not _arrival`). Snapshots carry `arrival/origin/origin_height`. Test: `Godot --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 tools/test_boss_entrance.tscn`.
- **Entrance screen** (`BossIntro`, src/ui/boss_intro.gd + assets/shaders/boss_intro.gdshader, sounds `py -3 tools/audio_boss_intro.py` -> assets/audio/boss_intro/): started by the boss itself on every peer whose local player stands within `intro.range` (700 px) of the arena (`_start_intro`; a late joiner starts it from the snapshot with `skip`). Data `intro` {name, epithet, range} in data/enemies.json. About 5 s of flight plus 4 s of card, game time, own CanvasLayer 55 (above the HUD, under the death screen). **Flight:** two bars of shadow smoke slam shut from top and bottom in 0.26 s (ragged flickering lip, violet edge, shadow cast into the picture), the world drains into cold violet, "shadow lightning" (the picture turns negative in organic blobs for 3-7 frames, cracks of light, glitch rows; never more than about three a second), a dithered dark closes in round the boss, the camera zooms to 1.5, anticipates the pillar and then rides with the boss (it never leaves the frame) and settles on the pillar. **Card:** at the touchdown white-violet flash + ring of pressure that bends the picture, the name condenses out of the smoke in the shadow face (`VoidTitle`, docs/void_face.md: razor letters of black flesh with a violet rim, pulsing magenta veins, glimmer, melting tendrils; it stutters at the touchdown), sparks run along its baseline with the front, a crack of light tears open under it (forks, beats with `VoidGlow`), the epithet condenses in the small face, now and then a glitch band slips; then the name crumbles upwards, the bars open, and the **boss bar tears open** (`_update_bar` waits for `_intro == null`). The player is held (`story_lock`, `InputManager.ui_blocking`), the HUD hidden; `_restore` gives everything back, also if the intro is removed or the boss/player vanishes (hard cap 16 s). Headless runs skip it. Capture: `Godot --rendering-driver opengl3 --path . --fixed-fps 60 tools/preview_boss_intro.tscn -- --dir=/abs/folder` (frames of the flight and of the card, plus a check that lock/HUD/camera/bar come back).
- Spawned only by that trigger (data/ground.json `arenas[].boss` {species, pillar}); not saved, so a fresh load hides it again. Loot is a placeholder.

## The fight, phase 1 (`mornath_fight.gd`, data `boss_sky_cleaver.fight`, test `tools/test_boss_fight.tscn`)
Host decides, every peer replays: `MornathFight` (a child of the boss) sends reliable RPCs (`_rpc_act`, `_rpc_lock`, `_rpc_break` ...), every peer plays the same event timeline (wings, feathers, warnings, sounds); only the host hurts. The boss's body is driven by the fight in `State.ACT` (`puppet_pos` / `puppet_height`); the chase is the one thing streamed (`_rpc_track`, unreliable, 20 Hz). A late joiner gets `_request_sync` (barrier built, phase, broken pillars).
- **Feathers** (`VoidFeather`, `src/fx/void_feather.gd`): small feathers rasterised at any angle (shaft, barbs at 45 degrees, comb edge, a split, violet rim, glowing shaft and threads of light along the barbs; contact sheet `Godot --headless --script tools/preview_feathers.gd -- --out=/abs.png`). `FeatherField` (`feather_field.gd`) holds everything on the floor: warnings (`feather_zones.gdshader`: lanes, discs and rings of flowing red pixels, cut to the arena's stone mask, denser and whiter as the hit comes; the area drawn is the area that hurts), falling and flying feathers (thrown ones leave fast: `ease_out`), impacts, the eye over the chased player. Every feather that is shot leaves a **wing blade tip** (`SkyCleaverRig.blade_tips`) and the wing beats then (`_kick_wing`).
- **Opening** (after the entrance screen): he rises, both wings up, and throws eight small feathers each into the ramp in the south and the rock steps in the west (data `ground.json` `arenas[].boss.barriers`); they plant themselves (`StuckFeather`: solid, infect the ground, white flashes run along the shaft) and block the ways; they dissolve when he dies or the fight resets.
- **Acts** (rotation `shot, shot, rain, shot, chase, move`, a gap of 0.5-1 s between; every time and number is in `fight` of data/enemies.json): RAIN (three waves, each cols/rows/diagonals from the left/right/top/bottom or rings, 52-64 % of the floor, safe strips wander; both wings up, droops 3 s after = armour x1.7), SHOT (left wing on the screen: one feather at a player, aim locks 0.3 s before the release, hits on its way, sticks, bursts after 0.9 s and leaves a zone; co-op: one more feather per player up to 3) and LINE (right wing: a lane through the locked spot across the arena, segments hit outwards, feathers plant themselves in it), CHASE (he follows a player's footsteps 0.55 s behind at 90 px/s, an eye over the player's head; a ring of 60 px, a burst of feathers out of the wings, he lands tired for 2.8 s: armour x2), BLAST (a player who clings to his pillar for 1.3 s gets a wing slam of 84 px). Charge colours: rain white-violet, shot magenta, line cold white, chase dark pulse (`SkyCleaverRig.charge_kind`).
- **End of every phase**: `HealthComponent.floor_value` holds him at the next third (+0.5 so the bar's fork does not fall yet). On reaching it he breaks off (everything announced is dropped, he roars), flies to the pillar of the phase (data `phases[].pillar`; phase 1: the east one, index 1; -1 = any left), rests there (`Pose.SPENT`, not hittable) and the pillar can be struck (`ArenaPillar.set_armed`, 70 wear; cracks of light, chips, `worn`). When it is gone it bursts (chunks cut from its own picture tumble and stay as rubble, a jagged stump stays, `shattered`) and he falls (`Pose.CRASHED` then `Pose.DOWNED`, lying on the ground). The first blow on him is the finisher (hit stop, white flash, shock ring, the health is set to 64 % so the bar's fork shatters), he gets up and flies to another pillar; the next phase's floor is held. **Phase 2 and 3 are not built: after the first break the same rotation goes on** (the second break exists in data, with any pillar left).
- **Reset**: if nobody alive is within 460 px of the arena for 8 s the level removes the boss (`reset_requested` -> `WorldLevel._reset_arena_boss`), the feathers dissolve, broken pillars stand again (`ArenaPillar.restore`) and the arena is armed again. A player who dies cannot come back through the feathers until the fight ends.
- Sounds: `py -3 tools/audio_sky_cleaver.py` (eighteen cues). Capture of every act: `Godot --rendering-driver opengl3 --path . --fixed-fps 60 tools/preview_boss_fight.tscn -- --dir=/abs [--acts=barrier,rain,shot,line,chase,blast,break] [--at=x,y]`.

### Speed and cost (second pass)
- **Faster:** warnings 0.95 s (rain), the aim of a shot locks 0.25 s before the release, charge 0.6 s, the chase follows 0.4 s behind at 98 px/s (the sprint is 104) and times out after 3.2 s, the opening takes 3.9 s, flights between pillars 140 px/s, hover 0.4-0.8 s. Rotation `shot, rain, shot, chase, shot, rain, move`.
- **Smaller and more:** barrier feathers 34-40 px (eight to a way), rain feathers 20x7 (14 to a strip, a ring every 16 px), thrown ones 22x7, shot 26x9, the salvo of the chase 16x6 (30 + 4 per extra player), the line plants one per segment (10 segments).
- **Cost** (measured with `tools/preview_boss_fight.tscn`, which prints `PERF` lines: script ms and draw calls per act): the fight and the feather field cost 0.05-0.5 ms a frame; the rig (9 ms a rebuild) is the expensive part and is rebuilt 60 times a second only while something moves fast (`MornathFight.wants_fast`), else 30 (about half the cost while he charges, rests or lies). The picture cache (`VoidFeather.warm_list`, about 180 pictures) is filled in the background from the moment the boss spawns (2.2 ms a frame budget), so nothing is drawn for the first time in an attack. The field draws nothing and pushes nothing to the shader while nothing is announced; falling feathers sit in one bucket per picture and lines and squares are drawn before the pictures so the canvas merges them (a rain peaks at about 60 extra draw calls, not 230); lists are pruned in place; stuck feathers have no light, only every third infects the ground and every second raises dust.


## The fight, phase 2: "Der Himmel reisst" (`MornathFight`, data `fight.transition`, `fight.rift`, `fight.p2`, `fight.judgement`; tests `tools/test_boss_fight.tscn` and `-- --success`)
Starts with the first finisher (health cut to 64 %). Everything is host-decided and replayed on every peer like phase 1; `phase >= 1` switches the numbers of an act to `fight.p2` (`_sp(group)`), the rotation (`pincer+`, `rain`, `hands`, `chase`, `cage`, `pincer`, `rain`, `move`, ... a trailing `+` = the next act follows after 0.06 s), the gap between acts (0.3-0.7 s) and, below 45 % health, 10 % more speed (`haste`: the fight's and the field's clocks run faster, sent with every act).
- **Transition** (`Mode.TRANSITION`, about 4.5 s, `BossPhaseScreen`: the bars of the entrance screen, shadow lightning, camera pushes in, "DER HIMMEL REISST" in the shadow face): his tendrils lift him (`rig.lift`), two wing blades shatter and grow back as light (`rig.burst_blades`), the look turns (`SkyCleaver.set_corruption` -> `rig.corruption`: 8 eyes, 10 tendrils, darker blades with magenta cracks, four light blades, a crack of white light in the chest, orbiting feather splinters, the broken halo; the voice gets deeper), he screams (`rig.crack_flash`, pressure wave: everybody within 200 px is thrown away), the rift tears open. The health is cut when the card ends.
- **Rift** (`RiftWall`): a solid tear in the sky: a dark slit up to ~50 px tall (tallest in the middle) with stars and rising streaks of light inside, torn basalt spikes and a bright thread along its top, a hot seam and low black teeth on the floor, shafts of light, drifting shards of broken sky, bolts of lightning; originally a jagged crack of void light between the two pillars that still stand, edge to edge of the floor (splits the arena). It hurts on touch (7 + knock back, a tick every 0.5 s); feathers and acts fly over it; the chase does not cross it. It **breathes**: every 25-30 s (the first after 15 s) it flickers for 1.1 s and then a gap of 78 px stays open for 4 s, the host sends an attack at once (`_hurry`) - so nobody is locked in one half, and a friend can come over. Late joiners get `rift_on` in `_sync`.
- **Acts:** PINCER (both wings up, left wing shoots an aimed feather at A while the right wing draws a **beam** through B, 0.18 s later they swap; alone both volleys go at you, the beam passes a little to one side), HANDS (a serrated blade of light turns from every pillar that stands like the hand of a clock, 120 degrees in 1.6 s, one clockwise, one against it; the fan of lanes lights up in the order of the sweep, a wedge of afterglow and scorched arcs stay behind it), CAGE (a ring of 36 feather stakes falls round a locked player at 92 px and closes to 30 px in 1.25 s, a 40 degree gap shows the way out and never faces the rift; a burst in the middle), RAIN II (waves mirrored over the rift: strips parallel to it, equally far on both sides; every second strip leaves a **puddle of void** that ticks for 1.5 s), CHASE II (112 px/s, 0.25 s behind, ring after 0.5 s with an **echo** ring 0.4 s later, a **shadow double** of the ring round the second player), BLAST II (two rings, 84 px then 92-152 px 0.34 s later).
- **Judgement** (`Mode.JUDGEMENT`): at 33 % he flies to the **northern pillar** (index 0), the rift collapses, he stands up unhurt and splits the sky. `JudgementFx`: a ring of 90 runes on the floor fills like a clock from the top, the sky goes violet-black (50 %), a column of white light grows above him; `JudgementMeter` under the boss bar: "URTEIL", a crack that runs out, the seconds. The pillar has 240 wear (x 1 + 0.7 per extra player) and 28 s; every 5 s it fights back (a shock wave r 90, 0.3 s later a wreath of feathers 112-140). **Pillar bursts in time:** the light flows out of him, white flash, he falls, finisher, phase 3 next (the health goes to 31 %, `fight.phases[1].to`). **Time runs out:** the light widens over the arena and everybody within 520 px dies (`HealthComponent.kill`); the next fight starts again at phase 2 (`SkyCleaver.checkpoint_reached` -> `WorldLevel._arena_phase`, spawn property `start_phase`: health at 64 %, the first pillar lies broken, the look has turned; the opening is thrown again and the transition replays).
- **Looks of the attacks** (`FeatherField`): `add_beam` (a thread of charging light runs from the wing tip to the start of the lane with motes and a closing ring, then the beam runs out along the lane: jagged hot core, violet or magenta body, a curtain above it, sparks off the floor, scorch left on the deck), `add_hand` (blade of light with lamellae like the wing blades, rotating rune hub, sparks off the tip), `add_cage` (stakes, a wall of light between them, two bright posts at the gap). Beams are also used by the first phase's LINE act.
- **Mood of the fight** (`BossAura`, `boss_aura.gdshader`, a quad under the HUD, local to every player within 480 px of the arena): the night gets heavier and colder (colours drain into violet), a ragged dark breathes in from the edges with the shadow heartbeat (magenta lip in phase 2), now and then the picture **glitches** (rows and blocks slip, red/blue split, violet specks, for a frame or two negative; every 4.5-9 s in phase 1, 3-6 s in phase 2, 1-3 s in the judgement) and at every big moment (blast, scream, pulse, beam, burst: `MornathFight._kick`); in the judgement the whole picture also breathes. `MornathFight.aura_level()`: 0.58 / 0.85 / 1.1-1.3.
- **Dev:** ENTWICKLER -> SPIELER -> **STARKES SCHWERT** (host): gives `sword_void` ("Leerenklinge", legendary, 30 damage, speed 1.15, reach 34; art in `tools/art_weapons.py`) and equips it. Capture of the acts: `tools/preview_boss_fight.tscn -- --dir=/abs --acts=barrier,break,pincer,hands,cage,rain2,judge` (`break` includes the transition). Look sheet: `docs/img/sky_cleaver_phase2.png`.
- Not built: phase 3, the suction of the rift (would need a hook in `player.gd`), blink over the rift is not blocked specially (the rift is solid for the blink's collision test like any wall).

### Balance and hitch pass (2026-11-08)
- Slower and heavier: gaps between acts 1.3-2.1 s (phase 2: 0.9-1.5 s), combo follow-up 0.8 s (was 0.06), rift-gap attack 0.6 s; warnings 15-35 % longer (rain waves 1.1-1.3 s apart, hands sweep 2.0 s, cage closes in 1.6 s, blast echo 0.55 s); damage about +50 % (shot/line 26-27, rain 24, chase 32, hands 27, cage 30, blast 20). Chase speed unchanged. All in `fight` of data/enemies.json.
- Hitches: the rig costs 6-10 ms a rebuild (`tools/bench_sky_cleaver.tscn`); fast ticks are 40 Hz now (were 60, which doubled the cost exactly when an attack began). `VoidFeather.warm_list` now covers all four variants of every flight and stuck size (a chase salvo drew ~30 feathers at 0.5-1.6 ms each in the middle of the attack). The zone shader is shown empty for four frames at setup (first-use compile), sound cues are loaded on a thread when the fight is created.

## The fight, phase 3: "Der Himmel brennt" (only the start is built; `MornathFight._begin_final_transition`, data `fight.transition3`)
Starts when the judgement's pillar has burst, he has fallen and the first blow (the finisher) landed (`phase` 2). About 8 s, `Mode.TRANSITION` with `_final` true, every peer plays it, the host hurts; the health is cut to 31 % when the card ends. Times (seconds of the transition clock, all in `transition3`):
- **0 - 1.7 s:** he breaks off, rises and flies to the **middle of the arena** (`SkyCleaver.arena_middle()`: the sun sigil, `ground.json` `arenas[].sigil`, default (26, -48); the middle tile is at the arena's southern edge, not its middle), hovering 64 px high, tendrils lift him.
- **1.9 s, the push:** a big wave (`ShockRing`, aura glitch, flash). Host: every living player within 560 px loses **25 % of her maximum health but never the last point** (`HealthComponent.take_survivable`: ignores the grace period, takes at most `current - 1`, a player at 1 HP is only knocked back) and is **launched along the ray away from the middle to the end of the floor** (`_end_of_floor`: last `FeatherField.on_floor` point 10 px short of the edge; `Player.launch(target, 0.75 s)` slides her there, fast then slowing, stopped by anything solid; she cannot act meanwhile).
- **3.0 s:** the wings spread (both raised, tendrils taut, charge builds). **3.3 s:** the glitching card starts (`BossPhaseScreen`, text **DER HIMMEL BRENNT**, `screen` block of `transition3`; `hot` 1 tints the whole shader pink-violet via the new `hot` uniform of `boss_intro.gdshader`, steady glitch rows on top of the lightning).
- **4.8 s, the scream** (card time 1.5 s): flash, `SkyCleaver.set_fury(1)` (the rig turns to its last form), the blades shatter again, pressure ring, `ArenaFury.ignite` (1.4 s). Card leaves, control returns; he flies to the last pillar (`_rise_fly`). `checkpoint_reached(2)`: a wipe in phase 3 restarts at this transition (`start_phase` 2; `_finish_barrier` -> `_rpc_start_phase_three`). Late joiners get the fire at once (`_sync`).
- **Look of the last form (`SkyCleaverRig.fury`, 0..1, eased in 1.25 s):** pink-violet fire on some blade tips, the crest and the back (`_build_fire` / `_draw_fire`, `PixelFlames.flame`, each flame burns for about two thirds of its own beat); every glow colour moves towards pink (`_hot`, rims and edges of the body towards magenta in `_tint`); wings a sixth longer, seven of the eleven blades per wing are blades of light (`FURY_LIGHT`: primaries 0, 2, 4, secondaries 7, 9 on top of 1, 3), 14 instead of 6 fragments circle him (eight new, bigger and faster, `SPL_*` arrays), more cracked blades; the voice gets deeper. `aura_level` 1.0.
- **The arena burns (`ArenaFury`, `GroundDecal.ignite`, `ArenaPillar.ignite`):** the inlaid turquoise light turns pink-violet and burns partly: `fury_ground.gdshader` recolours every turquoise pixel of the lit floor / pillar picture, `fury_teal_glow.gdshader` (additive, unshaded, a copy of the same picture showing only those pixels) makes them burn with white-hot patches that flare on their own beats, `fury_glow.gdshader` does the same for the glow sheets; `PixelFlames` stands flame clusters on parts of the light (about 40 on the floor, 4-9 on a pillar), each burning for a while, dying down and catching again; the pillar's light pool turns pink. Only drawn near a local player. It goes out when the boss dies (4 s) or the fight resets (`restore`).
- Not built: attacks of phase 3 (after the card the phase-2 rotation goes on without the rift, he is confined to the one pillar that still stands), the end of the fight, loot/story hooks. Test: `tools/test_boss_fight.tscn -- --success` (includes the push, the quarter of health, the fire); capture: `tools/preview_boss_fight.tscn -- --dir=/abs --acts=phase3 --skip-intro` (starts the fight at phase 3 through the checkpoint, pictures every `--every` frames).

### Phase 3: the attacks and the burn status (concept and open parts: docs/boss_phase3_konzept.md)
Numbers in `fight.p3` (layered over `p2` and the base, `MornathFight._sp`): pauses 0.55-0.95 s, rotation `dive, rain, dive, dive, rain`, below 20 % health everything 8 % faster, damage +~15 %.
- **The long rain** (`rain` in phase 3, `_rain_waves_final`): seven waves: vertical strips, rings from the sigil, diagonal strips leaning left (sweeping left), rings from a second point (-85, 40), diagonal strips leaning right, rings from a third point (80, -60), horizontal strips. New shape kinds `diag_l` / `diag_r`, `rings` takes an origin (`rain_shapes(kind, dir, phase_k, origin)`). Damage 28, warn 0.9 s, 0.95 s between waves, droop 2.0 s at armour 1.5; every wave covers 51-62 % of the floor.
- **The dive** (`Act.DIVE`, `_start_dive` / `_host_dive_step` / `_rpc_dive_commit`; rig `Pose.STOOP`, `rig.dive` 0 = aiming, 1 = diving): he leans forward on the pillar (0.95 s, an eye over the target), launches (150 -> 400 px/s, homing at 3.4 rad/s, 16 px over the floor, not hittable), rears up 34 px in front of the target and announces a ring (radius 34) for **0.32 s** only; the wings close: 36 damage, knock 160, **burn 5 s**. After the grab he stands open 1.3 s (armour 1.5); if it hit nobody (`FeatherField.hurt_total` did not move) 2.4 s at armour 2.0. The path is host-decided and streamed (`_rpc_dive_track` 20 Hz, unreliable) like the chase; the grab is a reliable RPC.
- **Status effects** (`StatusEffects`, `data/status_effects.json`, `HealthComponent.take_tick` / `ticked`, `BurnFx`, HUD `StatusTags`): see the concept doc; `FeatherField.add_ring/add_lane(..., burn)` sets a player alight, `damage_scale` of the boss scales the tick. Test: `tools/test_boss_fight.tscn -- --success` covers the burn, a grab that hits, a grab that is blinked out of and the long rain; capture `--acts=phase3,dive3`.

### Pillar marker ("smash the pillar")
Whenever a pillar is armed (the rest at the end of a phase, the judgement) it carries a `PillarMarker` (`src/entities/props/pillar_marker.gd`, child `Marker` of `ArenaPillar`, unlit, local to every peer with a living local player within 700 px; it reads `is_armed()` / `integrity`, which are replicated already, so nothing new is sent): (1) a dashed gold ring on the floor round the foot, where the hurtbox is, with ripples running inwards; (2) three chevrons pouring down the shaft; (3) a bark-paper leaf on a thread high over the capital (above the boss's head) with the words **SÄULE ZERSCHLAGEN**, a gold bud and, under the words, a crack of light whose length is what is left of the pillar (jumps shorter and flashes white with every blow, `ArenaPillar.worn`). In the judgement `MornathFight._drive_judgement` feeds `ArenaPillar.set_marker_urgency` the meter's clock: the whole marker turns magenta and the leaf stutters in the last seconds. It fades in with `set_armed(true)` and out at the burst / `set_armed(false)`. Tuning constants (`LIFT`, `RING_RX/RY`, `CHEVRON_Y`, `TEXT`) are at the top of the script.

### Sky cleave (replaces the beams)
The cosmetic beams of LINE and PINCER are now the *Himmelsspalt* (`FeatherField._draw_cleave`, still `add_beam`; damage is unchanged, the lanes): the wing draws a hairline crack along the lane while it is announced, at the hit it tears open into a lens of black sky as wide as the lane (cold or magenta lips with basalt teeth, a violet-to-magenta sky inside, hot seam, streaks of light rising), holds 0.45 s, snaps shut with dust and leaves a scar that fades. Same language as the rift wall (which now sinks to a low crack at its pillars so they stay visible, and has basalt plates and a sky gradient). The HANDS blades are untouched.

## Phase 3: glut, splitter, mark, the burning floor and the last judgement

Built on `FireArena` (`src/entities/enemies/fire_arena.gd`, child of the boss, `field.blaze`; its own real-time clock, cosmetics on every peer, only the host sets alight with `StatusEffects` `burn` or the effect `brandmark`). Numbers in `fight.p3` (`glut`, `splitter`, `mark`, `floor`) and `fight.judgement2`; the text of the design is in docs/boss_phase3_konzept.md ("Wie A, B und C gebaut sind"). Rotation `dive, rain, glut, splitter, dive, mark, rain, glut, dive, splitter, mark, rain`.
- **Act.GLUT**: fire feathers thrown at the players and around them, `blaze.light` (warn ring from the aim, ignite 0.9 s after the landing, burns 6 s, `burn` with scale 0.6, no direct damage).
- **Act.SPLITTER**: columns of light at the place a player stood `lag` seconds ago (host trail `_trail`, every second strike mirrored through `arena_middle()`), `blaze.column` (hairline, column, shards, embers 3 s) and a damage ring of the `FeatherField`.
- **Act.MARK**: a feather brands one player, `blaze.seal` over her head counts `total` s (HUD effect `brandmark`), `_host_mark_lock` sinks the seal into the floor `lock_before` s before the end (the ring of `radius` is then fixed and hurts + burns at the burst).
- **Burning floor**: under 20 % health `_rpc_edge` ignites the rim (`FireArena.set_edge`, `edge_width` px, `in_edge_band` by probing the floor mask); in the last judgement `_arm_floor` lets the whole floor burn (`set_floor`) and sets `cold_islands` (nothing burns there, `burn` is put out). Burning ticks every 0.4 s on the host; scale 0.5 (`floor`), the smallest tick of `StatusEffects` now scales with it.
- **Last judgement**: `fight.phases[2]` (10 %, pillar 2), `_judge_spec()` returns `judgement2` from phase 2 on, `_last_stand` after the pillar breaks (floor 0, armour 2.0, no finisher, a scream after 14 s), then he can die. A wipe restarts at the start of phase 3 (a checkpoint before the judgement is not built).
