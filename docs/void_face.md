# The shadow face (`VoidTitle`)

The letters of everything that belongs to the void, above all **boss names** (entrance screen, boss bar). Not a pixel font of blocks: tall, heavy letters of black void flesh that glimmer, morph and drip like alien goo. UI text stays `PixelText`.

- **Letterforms** (`py -3 tools/art_void_face.py`, glyph drawings in the script; writes `assets/ui/void_face_big.png` (cap height 44 px), `void_face_small.png` (17 px), `void_face_tiny.png` (11 px), `data/void_face.json`, preview `docs/img/void_face.png`): every glyph is a few strokes swept with variable width - a fat blade on one side, a thinner stroke on the other, bowls cut angular, feet and tails run out in thin necks that swell into drops. The sweep is fattened (`FAT`, `GROW`, `SIZE_WEIGHT`), blurred and cut again (`BLUR`, `CUT`: a metaball, so joins swell and corners melt). Rendered 8x oversampled and cut to hard pixels at the size the game shows them (**never scaled at run time**). Atlas channels: R letter, G signed distance to the edge (128 = edge, +-12 px), B the spine of the thick strokes. A-Z, Ä Ö Ü, `. , - ' ! ?` and a space. A new letter: add a `glyph(...)` in the script, run it, `Godot --headless --path . --import`.
- **Shader** (`assets/shaders/void_title.gdshader`, art pixel grid): black flesh, a little lighter towards the middle of a stroke; cold violet rim light on the edges that face the top left, a dim one on the others; the spine glows magenta-white and beats with the shadow creatures' double pulse (running outwards along the word); small stars flare up and die on the letters; an aura of violet dust breathes round them; the letter is its signed distance field cut at a level that breathes (the goo swells and shrinks, edges wobble), the picture is displaced by noise in whole pixels (more at the bottom), a wet shine runs just inside the rim, and **goo melts down** from every lower edge as 2 px stems with a drop at the end (`detail`/`goo` scale all of it down for the small faces). `reveal` 0..1.4: the letters condense out of smoke, left to right (a ghost of dither first); `dissolve` 0..1: they rise and crumble upwards; `glitch`: rows slip, colours split magenta/teal; `power` (how alive), `drip`.
- **Node** `VoidTitle` (src/ui/void_title.gd, a `ColorRect`): `setup(text, face, seed)` composes the word into one texture with room round it (cached per face and text), `set_look(reveal, dissolve, glitch, power, drip)`, `place(cap_left_top)` puts the capitals where you want them (`ink_size`, `lead`, `cap_top`, `baseline` are the metrics). It runs its own clock (pausable).
- **Used by** `BossIntro` only (name in the big face, epithet in the small one, both blacker via `set_ink(1.0)`: a black outline round the letters and a dimmer rim; the crack of light between them; the shock of the touchdown makes the word stutter, every glitch band of the card too). The boss bar no longer uses it: its name (MORNATH) is plain `PixelText` in gold, like the judgement's URTEIL. `set_ink` exists for any word on a busy picture.
- Preview: `Godot --rendering-driver opengl3 --path . --fixed-fps 60 tools/preview_void_face.tscn -- --dir=/abs/folder` (smoke, forming, full, glitch, crumbling, gone).
